# Chariz Interiors

Chariz Interiors is the website and online shop for an interior design company. Clients can explore the company's past projects, book a design consultation, start a new project and buy home decor products online — all in one place.

## Business idea

Interior design clients want to see a designer's work before they commit, and they often want to buy the pieces they see. Chariz Interiors turns the company's portfolio into a sales channel:

- **Show the work** — a gallery and project pages showcase residential and commercial interiors.
- **Win new clients** — visitors book paid consultations or submit a "start a project" brief.
- **Sell products** — a shop sells decor items with cart, wishlist and online payment.
- **Keep clients engaged** — customers have accounts with orders, billing and notifications.

Revenue comes from design projects, consultation fees and product sales.

## Key features

- Home page with interior design categories, banners and team section
- Services, about, gallery and project showcase pages
- Consultation booking and "start a project" forms
- Shop with product details, cart and wishlist
- Paystack checkout, payment methods and order history
- User accounts: sign up, login, account details, billing, notifications and settings
- Downloadable PDFs and offline storage of data (Dexie/IndexedDB)

## Tech stack

- React, built with Vite
- Redux Toolkit, Axios
- Material UI, Tailwind CSS and shadcn/ui
- Formik + Yup
- Paystack (`react-paystack`)
- Deployed on Vercel (`vercel.json`)
